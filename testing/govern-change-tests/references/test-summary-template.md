# Test Summary Template

Use this as a compact starting point for multi-risk/real-material work. Reuse project sections and identifiers; omit irrelevant sections. Fill with facts, not placeholder pass counts. Keep detailed cases and errors in their existing canonical locations.

## Current conclusion

State what is verified, what is pending or failed, exact candidate/fingerprint, report time/timezone, and local/CI/live/manual boundaries. Point to the latest checkpoint when earlier sections contain superseded results.

## Materials and scope

| Material ID/group | Role and confirmation basis | Project/package/subject/version | Hash or private manifest | Original/derived/copy | Inspected/parsed/executed scope | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Count unique content and executed scenarios separately. Role inspection is not full document analysis. Preserve unresolved pairings and unsupported formats.

## Risk and test coverage

| Risk/error ID | Observable contract | Material/case IDs | Unit/integration/document/browser layer | Expected-result authority | Executable path/command | Actual status/candidate | Missing coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Historical errors and causes

| Error ID | Observation and category | Cause/certainty/evidence | Attempt and rationale | Actual result/current freshness | Prevention and linked case |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep a history-link column or link separate canonical errors. Include failed approaches and corrected hypotheses that affect the next repair; do not copy full transcripts or present old failures as current reproductions.

## Execution evidence

| Run/date | Exact command | Environment/candidate/fingerprint | Input hash/scope | Passed/failed/error/skipped/timeout | Evidence locator | What this proves / does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Report generated vs actually executed cases, pre-fix vs post-fix, and first run vs rerun. Do not sum overlapping suites or silently promote partial/final-delta checks into a complete final-candidate run.

## Repair and manual acceptance

Link the repair plan, pending discussion decisions, relevant skills, and tests to create/renew. Give repeatable manual steps and expected visible results for each step; include source clicks, save/reload, version changes, and fallback provenance where relevant. Do not require production actions before the applicable approval.

## Remaining limits and next gate

List genuinely unverified business facts, material groups, formats, OCR, real providers, databases, concurrency, independent review, CI and deployment. Define the next gate without claiming unexecuted checks passed.
